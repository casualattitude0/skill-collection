ponytail lens (keep the strongest, disable the rest) before they cost more
tokens/trigger collisions than they're worth.

  and not part of this group.

  reference guide), `playwright-cli` (microsoft, interactive browser automation),
  `playwright-generate-test` (github-awesome-copilot, generate-from-live-site),
  `webapp-testing` (anthropics, Python/local-app debugging), and
  `frontend-testing-best-practices` (sergiodxa, framework-agnostic principles).
  Five skills sharing "playwright"/"testing" trigger words — check whether all
  five earn their place or some are redundant with each other before the next
  audit.
